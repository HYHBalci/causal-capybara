# CATE projection, calibration and GATES require honest evaluation

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

A CATE is a conditional average contrast, not an observed individual treatment effect. BLP summarizes it through specified features, calibration evaluates learned predictions, and GATES summarizes groups induced by a learned score. Keep their estimands and nulls distinct. Formal guarantees require the stated score construction, independence or sample-splitting and covariance procedure; plotting bins or regressing fitted predictions does not by itself implement the published estimator. Report simplifications of the local adapter and distinguish exploratory diagnostics from validated inference.
