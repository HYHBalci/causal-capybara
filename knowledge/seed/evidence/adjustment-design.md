# Adjustment depends on causal role and timing

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

A variable can predict outcomes and still be inappropriate for adjustment. Conditioning on a mediator can remove part of a total effect; conditioning on a collider can open a noncausal path. Choose the adjustment set using the stated causal graph and pre-treatment measurement timing. Complete-case restrictions and missing-data procedures also need explicit assumptions and may change which population the comparison represents.
