# Subgroup discovery and confirmatory analysis have different evidence requirements

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Define baseline modifiers and the family of hypotheses before looking for outcome patterns. Report all attempted modifiers, scales and horizons, with a multiplicity strategy or explicit exploratory status. Preserve main effects when adding treatment interactions. Baseline timing and prespecification declarations are study assertions to audit, not facts verified by a checkbox. Rejecting one omnibus null neither adjusts pointwise subgroup intervals nor establishes every subgroup difference.
