# Treatment-confounder feedback requires sequential reasoning

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

When a measured covariate is affected by earlier treatment and predicts later treatment and outcome, ordinary covariate adjustment can fail to target the intended treatment-history effect. Define the intervention history and order its measurements. Marginal structural models, longitudinal g-computation and structural-model approaches use different estimation constructions, but all require their applicable sequential exchangeability, consistency and support conditions; fitted histories do not verify those assumptions.
