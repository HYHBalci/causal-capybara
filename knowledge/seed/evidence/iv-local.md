# IV interpretation is a compliance-specific identification statement

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

A relevant instrument is not automatically a valid instrument. Exclusion, independence and the applicable compliance restrictions connect an instrument-induced comparison to a local effect. Under the usual binary monotonicity setup, that target concerns compliers rather than necessarily every eligible unit. Report the instrument construction, first-stage information and weak-identification-aware uncertainty; a different estimator such as LIML does not establish exclusion.
