# Ranking tests assess a rule with a specified evaluation design

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

RATE and its AUTOC or Qini weighting summarize treatment-effect differences along a prioritization rule. A valid evaluation design separates construction of the ranking from its evaluation outcomes. Holding an estimated ranking fixed during row bootstrap omits learning uncertainty unless the inferential target is explicitly conditional on an independently learned rule. A non-significant ranking test can mean limited power or a poor rule, even when true effects vary. Do not equate it with evidence of no heterogeneity.
