# Fixed-profile standardization inference

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original methodological explanation; agent contextual review only.

A delta-method contrast based on fitted-parameter covariance conditions on the empirical profiles used for standardization. Inference for a superpopulation target additionally needs the relevant uncertainty from estimating its covariate distribution. Explicitly distinguish subgroup-restricted averages from controlled comparisons setting a continuous modifier to chosen values.
