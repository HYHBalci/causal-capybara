# RD targets depend on the cutoff design and estimator

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Regression discontinuity is a local design. Continuity-based, local-randomization, fuzzy and kink analyses rely on different conditions and targets. Inspect the running variable, threshold rule, treatment discontinuity, bandwidth and uncertainty procedure. Density and covariate-balance diagnostics can reveal problems, but a failure to reject cannot establish all required smoothness or assignment conditions. Extrapolation away from the cutoff needs an additional argument.
