# Survival and RMST contrasts are horizon-specific population targets

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

The survival contrast S^1(tau) minus S^0(tau) compares event-free probabilities at a declared horizon. RMST is the integral of survival from zero through tau, so an RMST difference has duration units and requires the same horizon in both strategies. A conditional hazard ratio or AFT coefficient is a different summary. Standardized survival predictions average strategy-specific curves over the declared target covariate distribution under the needed treatment, censoring and model conditions.
