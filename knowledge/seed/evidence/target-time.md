# Eligibility and follow-up must share a defensible time zero

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

An exposure label constructed from events after eligibility can require units to survive until they receive that label. Counting that guaranteed event-free period as exposed follow-up introduces immortal-time bias. Specify eligibility, assignment or strategy classification, and start of follow-up together; inspect joins and exclusion rules for future information. The appropriate emulation depends on the intended strategy, not only on available timestamps.
