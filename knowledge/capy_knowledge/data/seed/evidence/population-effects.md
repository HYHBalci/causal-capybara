# Interference and transport alter the comparison

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Cross-unit treatment effects require an exposure mapping or allocation strategy, so direct, spillover and overall effects can differ. Transport to another population additionally requires source and target definitions, relevant effect modifiers and support. A balancing weight or network summary alone does not establish the assumptions needed for either question. State which units, assignments and population distributions the reported effect averages over.
