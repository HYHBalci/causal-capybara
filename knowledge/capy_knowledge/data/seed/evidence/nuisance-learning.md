# Orthogonal learning retains identification and inference conditions

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

An orthogonal score can reduce first-order sensitivity to nuisance estimation errors; it does not remove confounding. Fit nuisance models on training folds and evaluate target-specific scores on held-out observations, keeping dependent units or clusters in valid split units. ATE and ATT scores are not interchangeable. Valid uncertainty also requires the stated rate, sampling and dependence conditions, even when one nuisance component gives a doubly robust consistency property.

Source context: bang2005, chernozhukov2018.

This note does not certify a study, a complete implementation, or independent human review.
