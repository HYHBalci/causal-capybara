# Outcome families require distinct interaction targets

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

An identity-link interaction concerns a conditional mean difference, a logit interaction concerns conditional log odds ratios, and a log-link interaction concerns conditional mean ratios. Standardized risk or mean differences are different functionals and require contrasts of their own predictions and covariance. A count-rate model additionally defines exposure and an offset; compare predictions at a stated exposure. Robust standard errors cannot repair a wrong mean model, inadequate overlap or unmeasured confounding.
