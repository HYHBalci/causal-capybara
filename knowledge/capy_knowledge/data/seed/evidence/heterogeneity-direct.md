# Heterogeneity requires a direct comparison on a declared scale

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Compare subgroup treatment contrasts directly; do not compare their separate significance labels. A joint Wald test needs an estimable contrast vector and covariance of full rank. Its null is equality on the stated effect scale, for the stated population and horizon. Failure to reject is not an equivalence result, and rejecting the null does not establish causal identification.
