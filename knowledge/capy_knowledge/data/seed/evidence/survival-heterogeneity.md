# KM and RMST heterogeneity depend on common horizons and subgroup support

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Compare KM survival differences at a fixed horizon or RMST differences integrated through the same horizon across baseline subgroups. Raw within-cell KM requires a defensible censoring assumption within each treatment-by-subgroup cell; conditioning only in a later interpretation does not adjust that estimator. Estimate the joint covariance of the subgroup contrasts, preserve the sampling unit when resampling, and disclose failed replicates. A log-rank test is not a test of equality of those fixed-horizon treatment contrasts.
