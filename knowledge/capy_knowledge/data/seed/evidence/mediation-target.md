# Mediation targets require additional assumptions

SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Original Causal Capybara explanation; agent contextual review only. External publication bodies are not included.

Randomized treatment does not automatically identify mediator effects. A controlled direct effect fixes a mediator intervention, while natural effects involve mediator potential outcomes and additional cross-world or equivalent structural conditions. Interventional mediation effects specify different stochastic mediator strategies. Identify the target explicitly, inspect mediator-outcome confounding and exposure-induced confounders, and avoid relabeling a mediator-adjusted treatment regression coefficient as any of these effects without its identification argument.
